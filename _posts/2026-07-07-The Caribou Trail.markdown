The Caribou Trail is a first-person historical narrative adventure following a group of Newfoundland soldiers during the First World War. Players explore the battlefield, confront the dangers of the front, and experience a deeply personal story about friendship, survival, and psychological trauma as reality gradually begins to unravel.

## My Contribution

The Caribou Trail was originally developed by **Unreliable Narrators**.

I did not participate in the original development of the game. My contribution was exclusively focused on porting the existing project to **PlayStation 5** while working at **Wild Blueberry Games** on behalf of Unreliable Narrators.

My role involved adapting the project to the PS5 platform, resolving platform-specific issues, improving performance, handling compliance requirements, and preparing the game for console submission.

> **Role:** PS5 Porting Programmer  
> **Employer:** Wild Blueberry Games  
> **Client studio:** Unreliable Narrators  
> **Platform:** PlayStation 5

***

## PS5 Port

My main responsibility was to bring the existing version of The Caribou Trail to PlayStation 5 without changing its original creative direction or core game design.

The porting work included:

- Integrating and adapting the project for PlayStation 5
- Diagnosing and resolving platform-specific issues
- Upgrading Unreliable Narrators' custom Unreal Engine build
- Migrating custom engine modifications to a newer Unreal Engine version
- Ensuring compatibility with PlayStation SDK requirements
- Resolving engine migration, compilation, and platform compatibility issues
- Performing CPU and GPU profiling
- Performing memory profiling and investigating memory usage
- Identifying and resolving performance bottlenecks
- Optimizing the game toward a stable target of **60 FPS on PlayStation 5**
- Handling PS5 Technical Requirements Checklist compliance
- Supporting testing and submission preparation
- Validating the game in its PlayStation 5 environment

***

## Unreal Engine Upgrade

As part of the port, I upgraded Unreliable Narrators' custom Unreal Engine build to a newer engine version required for compatibility with the PlayStation SDK.

Because the studio was using a customized version of Unreal Engine, this was not a straightforward engine update. I had to migrate and reintegrate the studio's custom engine modifications into the newer version while resolving compilation errors, API changes, and platform-specific compatibility issues.

Once the upgrade was complete, I validated that the project and its custom engine features continued to function correctly on PlayStation 5.

***

## Performance and Memory Profiling

A major part of the port involved extensive CPU, GPU, and memory profiling.

I analyzed the game's performance on PS5, identified bottlenecks, and implemented optimizations to help the game achieve a stable target of **60 FPS**.

This work included investigating frame-time spikes, expensive systems, rendering costs, and platform-specific performance issues. I also performed detailed memory profiling to monitor memory usage, identify excessive allocations, and resolve memory-related problems that could affect stability on console.

The profiling and optimization process required repeated testing directly on PS5 hardware to verify that changes produced consistent results in representative gameplay situations.

***

## PS5 TRC Compliance

I also handled compliance with Sony's **Technical Requirements Checklist**, commonly referred to as TRC.

This involved reviewing platform requirements, investigating compliance issues, implementing the necessary fixes, and validating expected behavior on PS5.

The TRC work covered platform-specific scenarios and behaviors required for submission, helping prepare the game for certification and release on PlayStation 5.

***

## Scope of My Work

All original gameplay, narrative, art, level design, and game systems were created by the team at Unreliable Narrators.

My involvement began during the console-porting phase and was limited to the PlayStation 5 version. I did not work on the original development of the game.

My contribution focused on:

- PS5 platform integration
- Custom Unreal Engine migration
